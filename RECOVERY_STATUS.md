## Integrated Algorithm
- `engine/matrix.py` builds the callee-first frontier from function inventory, assembly call targets, recovery status and the knowledge store.
- `engine/recovery_loop.py` selects a function and action, drafts through `engine/draft.py`, validates parse/compile, runs the configured oracle, records the outcome in the shared Q-table, experience replay and per-function history, and stops at the 25% yield floor.
- `engine/rl_engine.py` provides real epsilon-greedy Q-learning updates and reward shaping (`verified=1.0`, `compiled=0.25`, `failed=-0.5`).
- `engine/verify.py` exposes `verify_status()` with `verified`, `failed`, `skipped` and `not_verified`; skips are no longer reported as passes.
- `run_verification.py` writes truthful per-function status instead of claiming all 295 functions verified.
- `engine/bootstrap.py` turns frozen representation search into verified routine data with provenance, then records the outcome through the same knowledge and RL store.

## Promotion Rules
- `parse` and `compile` produce a candidate only.
- `dat-parity`, `constructor-parity` and format selftests never promote source.
- Byte/relocation/trace oracles promote only relpaths that actually matched.
- `routine-trace-parity` promotes only the exact routine whose training and terminal cases matched; a finite corpus does not prove every input or full-game parity.
- Function-level promotion requires the scoped routine oracle or equivalent behavioral witness.
- Solved routines feed later prompts and Q-learning; unsolved callees remain blockers.
